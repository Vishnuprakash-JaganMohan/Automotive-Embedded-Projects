## 👨‍💻 About This Repository

Welcome to my **Automotive Embedded Systems Lab** — a collection of self-driven projects created to explore automotive software development, embedded programming, and electronic control systems.

I am an **M.Sc. Embedded Systems student at TU Chemnitz, Germany**, with over four years of industrial R&D experience in embedded hardware and software development.

My goal is to expand my existing embedded engineering expertise into **automotive embedded software**, developing practical projects that demonstrate my understanding of software architecture, hardware-software interaction, and automotive communication systems.

> 🛠️ Every project is an opportunity to learn, experiment, debug, and engineer something better.
